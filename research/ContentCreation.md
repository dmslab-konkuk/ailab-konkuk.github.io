# Content Creation AI Research

**Lead Researcher: 김동희 (Donghee Kim)**

Our Content Creation research focuses on building **AI agents that transform documents and text into structured multimedia outputs** — bridging the gap between static information and dynamic, consumable content.

---

## PDF2Video Agent

**PDF2Video Agent** is a system that leverages Large Language Models (LLMs) to automatically convert the core content of PDF documents into storyboard data for video production, and then produces actual videos based on that data.

### Context-to-Script (문맥 기반 대본 추출)
Precisely analyzes text data within documents to generate narration scripts scene-by-scene — maintaining the expertise of the original text while making it accessible for viewers.

### Visual Directing (시각 연출 프롬프트)
Generates visual prompts that define the atmosphere and composition of each scene, serving as key indicators for video producers or AI image generation tools.

### Automated Storyboard Export (스토리보드 구조화)
Parses generated scripts and direction guides with precise delimiters, automatically saving them as structured documents (Excel), thereby digitizing the video planning pipeline.

### Video Production (영상 콘텐츠 제작)
Combines visual elements and narration based on the constructed storyboard data to produce the final video content.

## Topic Segmentation AI

**Topic Segmentation** is a technology that automatically detects context transition points within long text documents and classifies information into semantically coherent sections.

### Semantic Boundary Detection (의미 기반 경계 탐지)
Infers contextual flow to insert delimiters at points where topics change. Preserves the original content without summarization while establishing logical boundaries at the semantic level.

### Automatic Subtitle Assignment (자동 소제목 부여)
Automatically generates subtitles that capture the core content of each segmented section, enabling users to grasp the overall structure of extensive documents at a glance.

### Adaptive Data Refinement (데이터 적응형 정제)
Performs post-processing logic that merges fragmented sections with preceding content based on configured minimum length thresholds, optimizing information readability and density.
