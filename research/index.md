Our research is centered on **AI Agent systems** — autonomous intelligent entities that perceive, reason, plan, and act. We pursue three strategic research pillars that together form a comprehensive vision for next-generation agentic intelligence.

---

## [SDV-AI Agentic Intelligence Area](./SDV-AI-Agentic-Intelligence)

**Lead Researcher: 장성훈 (Sunghoon Jang)**

Research on **Software-Defined Vehicle (SDV) × AI Agent** convergence — deep learning-based perception, agentic decision-making, digital twins, and reinforcement learning-driven vehicle intelligence.

- SDV AI Agents for autonomous driving
- Digital Twin & Domain Adaptation via CARLA Simulator
- Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning for cooperative driving

<br/>

## [G.A²Framework — Core Research (세부 1)](./GA2Framework)

**Lead Researcher: 김청해 (Chunghae Kim)**

A universal agent framework (**G.A²Framework**) that unifies **Physical Agents** and **Thinking Agents** into a single composable architecture powered by DI-based modular design.

- Agent Core Engine, Perception, Cognition, Action, Learning & Memory
- Long-Term Context-based Memory & Knowledge Graph (GraphRAG)
- Dynamic Proactive Multi-Agent Collaboration

<br/>

## [Content Creation AI Area](./ContentCreation)

**Lead Researcher: 김동희 (Donghee Kim)**

AI-driven **content creation** research — transforming documents and text into structured multimedia outputs through intelligent agents.

- PDF2Video Agent: automated document-to-video production pipeline
- Topic Segmentation AI: semantic boundary detection and structuring
